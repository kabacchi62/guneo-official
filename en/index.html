ABOUT THE GAME

Play #GUNEO, a free one-tap browser arcade game! Tap to fly, dodge the walls, and beat your high score. Play instantly on mobile or desktop — no download required.

HOW TO PLAY

How to play the web demo

1. Choose your mode

Choose EASY or HARD. In HARD mode, you can also select your speed.

2. Tap to move

Tap to make GUNEO rise. Release to let it fall. Time your moves carefully.

3. Dodge the walls

Avoid the walls and try to beat your distance record!

Play instantly in a browser on your phone or computer.
